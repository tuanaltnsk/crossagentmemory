THE CONCEPT

What is cross-agent memory?

Cross-agent memory refers to infrastructure that allows autonomous AI agents to retain, retrieve, and share useful context across tasks, tools, sessions, and specialized roles.

As multi-agent systems become more capable, continuity between agents may become a defining layer of AI infrastructure. This domain expresses that concept directly.
